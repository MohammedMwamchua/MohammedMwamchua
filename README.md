## 🙋🏾‍♂️ About me

I'm a software developer from Tanzania. I like building things people actually use: a school website the staff can update on their own, a ledger a restaurant manager checks every night, a rental site where people find a house.

- 🎓 **Bachelor of Computer Engineering**, Dar es Salaam Institute of Technology (DIT), 2026
- 🎓 **Diploma in Computer Engineering**, Mbeya University of Science and Technology (MUST), 2023
- 🚆 Worked as a **SCADA engineer** at AGS on the SGR railway project (communication and signalling systems)
- 🏢 Field training at **TANESCO**, **NSSF** and **Dodoma City Council** (mobile apps, web, IT support)
- 🛠️ I also build systems for local businesses under the name **Leish Tech**
- 🗣️ I speak English and Swahili
