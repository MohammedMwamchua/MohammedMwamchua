## Hi, I'm Mohamed

I'm a software developer in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. I mostly build web apps with React, Django and PostgreSQL.

Most of what I build starts from a real problem around me. My restaurant needed a way to track daily sales and cash shortages, so I built Daftari. My old secondary school needed a website the staff could update without calling a developer, so I built that too.

I studied Computer Engineering at DIT (Bachelor's, 2026) and MUST (Diploma, 2023). Before that I did field training at TANESCO, NSSF and Dodoma City Council, and I worked as a SCADA engineer at AGS on the SGR railway (communication and signalling systems).

### Projects

- **[Daftari](https://github.com/MohammedMwamchua/daftari)**: bookkeeping app for a chips stall and restaurant. The manager closes each day, counts the cash, and it works out shortages, salaries and profit. Swahili and English. *Django REST, React, PostgreSQL*
- **[Panga Nasi](https://github.com/MohammedMwamchua/MKP)**: house rental listings for Tanzania. People browse houses and send rental requests. *React, Django, PostgreSQL*
- **[Mnadani Secondary School](https://github.com/MohammedMwamchua/mnadani-secondary-school)**: school website with an admin panel for news, photos, awards and alumni. *React, SQLite*
- **StrokeGuard**: hospitals register patients, check stroke risk with a Random Forest model, and send lifestyle advice by SMS. *FastAPI, Flutter, SQLite* (code not public yet)
- **[Portfolio](https://mohamed-mwamchua-portfolio.vercel.app)**: my personal site.
